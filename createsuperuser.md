## Createsuperuser SOP

Enter the following command:
> python manage.py createsuperuser

Follow the prompts:
- Username : Enter your account name
- Email address : Enter your Email address (You can leave it blank and press Enter)
- Password : Enter your password (the characters will not be displayed on the screen while you are typing)
- Password (again): Enter the password again to confirm

I personally recommend using a created account as the administrator account.
Information is as follows:

> Username: sysadmin
> Password: Depends on personal preference (I would personally set it to be the same as the account.)
